# Dragon Automaton: Build Notes

Model: `dragon_automaton.3dm` (generated by `dragon_automaton.py`; re-run it after changing any value).
Dragon: *Flexi Dragon* by D3Craft (MakerWorld, Standard Digital File License: personal use, credit them, don't redistribute). Print it as-is, coiled, at **125%** (already scaled: `FlexiDragon_D3Craft_125pct_PERSONAL_USE.stl`).

## Run direction
Turn the shaft **clockwise when viewed from the hand-crank (right) end**.
That way the body wave runs tail → head, and the head rises slowly over about 280° of each turn, then drops 18 mm over about 80°. The foot is flat, so it rides the cam's highest point; the drop is softer than the cam's 60° step.
Running it backwards drives the head follower up the steep face of the snail cam, which can jam it.

## Slicer settings (Bambu Studio)
- **X-Y hole compensation: leave at 0.** The clearances below already allow for the usual hole shrink. If you also turn on slicer compensation, holes end up oversized.
- **No supports needed for any part** in the orientations below.
- **Print every functional part in the same filament you used for the ladder.** Fits change between filaments.

## Print orientation
| Part | On the bed | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Wings (blue) | Membrane flat, bones up | Knuckle is flush with the underside and the lug is on top. Verified: nothing extends below the 6,932 mm² base face. |
| Wing bones/claws/scales (white) | With the wing (2-colour) or separately, glued | |
| Cradles | **Boss down** (pad up) | Boss sides flare at ≤42°, so no supports. Socket opens on the bed side. |
| Chest mount block | Base down | Ear holes are horizontal teardrops. |
| Cams | Flat face down, hub up | D-bore prints vertical, so it's round and accurate. |
| Box panels, A-frame, end walls | Laser cut, not printed | 1/8 in plywood; 6 mm parts are two layers glued (see PRINT_ME_FIRST.md). |
| Foot discs | Disc down | Glue socket opens upward. |
| Flexi dragon | As supplied (coiled) | |

## Fits (radial clearance per side, FDM PLA/PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle)
All of these live in one block at the top of `dragon_automaton.py` (`FIT_*`).

| Fit | Where | Design hole | Clearance | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `FIT_SHAFT_CAM` | Cam bores on 8 mm D-shaft | 8.4 mm D (flat at 3.7) | 0.20 | Slip, locked by M3 grub on the flat |
| `FIT_SHAFT_RUN` | Shaft through end walls + A-frame | 8.7 mm | 0.35 | Running |
| `FIT_ROD_GUIDE` | 6 mm dowels through top plate + guide bar | 6.8 mm | 0.40 | Sliding |
| `FIT_ROD_SOCKET` | Rod tops in cradle sockets | 6.8 mm slot | 0.40 | Rocking on 2 mm pin |
| `FIT_GLUE` | Posts in chest mount; dowels in foot sockets | 6.3 mm | 0.15 | Glued |
| `FIT_PIN_FREE` | Cradle cross-pin slot, wing knuckle, link lug | 2.5 / 3.5 / 2.5 mm | 0.25 | Free-turning (teardrop where horizontal) |
| `FIT_PIN_PRESS` | 3 mm hinge pins in chest ears | 3.2 mm | 0.10 | Press (+ drop of CA) |
| `FIT_PANEL_PIN` | 3 mm alignment pins in panel seams | 3.2 mm | 0.10 | Snug + glue (teardrop) |
| `M3_PILOT_R` | Grub screws in cam hubs | 2.6 mm | n/a | Self-tapping (screw OD − 0.4) |
| `PAD_INSET` | Cradle pad vs segment base | pad 1.0 mm narrower | 0.5 / side | Hidden glue pad |

Other clearances, all verified at 8 shaft angles:
- **Cradle slots:** ±4.5 mm. Worst-case margin including cradle tilt is **+0.7 mm** (R1, 135°).
- **Pads:** bevelled 1.5 mm at the ends. No contact with the dragon at any angle.
- **Wings:** no contact with the dragon, box, cradles or rods across −14° to 47.5°.
- **Wing crossbar:** at least 4.5 mm above the top plate and 1.5 mm below the chest mount.
- **Lowest cradle:** at least 14 mm above the top plate.

## Print the fit-test ladder first (~30 min, orange, in front of the box)
Each fit has 3 holes: **tight (−0.05) / design / loose (+0.05 mm)**, left to right. A notch marks the end of each group.
- **Base, vertical holes:** cam D-bore, shaft running, rod guide, post glue, rod socket, panel pin.
- **Wall, horizontal teardrop holes:** 2 mm free, 3 mm free, 3 mm press, panel pin.

For each group, find the hole that feels right and set that fit's `FIT_*` value:
- If it's the tight hole, subtract 0.05.
- If it's the loose hole, add 0.05.
- If none of the three works, step by another 0.05 and reprint just the ladder.

Then re-run the script; every part updates. Use your **actual** dowels and pins, not nominal sizes. Wooden dowels vary by batch, reportedly up to ±1 mm on cheap stock. Measure yours: if they aren't close to 6.0 mm, set `ROD_R` to half the measured diameter.

## Suggested order
1. Fit-test ladder (above). Update `FIT_*` and re-run.
2. Dragon at 125%. Joints get looser when a flexi model is scaled up. Flex it by hand through ±7 mm at the segments listed in the `FIT Cradle_*` lines.
3. One cam, one cradle and the A-frame. Test them on a short length of D-shaft and dowel.
4. Hand-crank a partial build: R3, R4 and the head cam only.
5. Laser-cut box: glue the doubled parts, then the box (steps in PRINT_ME_FIRST.md).

## Hardware (almost everything is printed now)
Made by `printed_hardware.py` (files `08_*`), run after `dragon_automaton.py`:
- **Camshaft:** two printed 8 mm D-shaft pieces (215.6 + 110.4 mm) joined by a glued sleeve between cams R3 and R4. Print the D flat on the bed with 100% infill. Checked: about 0.23 mm sag on the longest 157 mm span under 2 N, and 1.8° twist end to end.
- **Push rods** ×5, **wing rod with built-in crossbar**, **shoulder posts** ×2, **3 mm pins** ×12.
- **Cams:** the D-bore sets each cam's angle. Glue them in place with CA instead of using grub screws.
- **Assembly order for the shaft:** slide piece A in from the left through the wall, R1, R2, the wing cam, the A-frame and R3. Put the sleeve on piece B, then slide B in from the right through the wall, the head cam and R4. Slide the sleeve over the joint and glue it.

Use 1.75 mm filament for:
- the 2 mm cross pins (about 20 mm each, a drop of CA in the rod)
- the 2 wing links (bend L hooks 29 mm apart after warming with a hair dryer)

Still to buy: a 25 mm gearmotor (30–60 rpm), a barrel jack and a speed controller. CA glue. Dry lube (graphite or PTFE) on the cams and on the shaft where it runs through the walls.

## Known risks (not checkable in CAD)
- **Dragon joint range:** inferred, not verified.
- **Tail:** droops past the last cradle.
- **Head drop:** an 18 mm drop each turn. Add a small rubber bumper if it's noisy.
- **Electronics:** not designed yet; the speed controller is only a placeholder.
